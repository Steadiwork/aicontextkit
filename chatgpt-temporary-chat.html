What is a temporary chat in ChatGPT?

A temporary chat is a conversation that stays out of your chat history and isn't used to improve OpenAI's models while it remains temporary. You can still choose whether it uses your existing memories and custom instructions. Here's how it works, based on OpenAI's own help page.

Menu names and options can change, and some may not be in your account yet. Treat the steps below as signposts.

How to start a temporary chat

  1. Open a new chat.
  2. Select Temporary.
  3. Choose Personalized or Unpersonalized before you send your first message.
  4. Enter your message.

You can't change the personalization choice after the conversation starts.

Personalized vs Unpersonalized

Before the chat begins, OpenAI lets you pick how it should respond:

  • Unpersonalized: does not use memory, custom instructions, or plugins.
  • Personalized: can use your existing memories, custom instructions, and plugins.

Neither option creates or updates memories while the chat stays temporary. Account and workspace restrictions still take priority over that choice.

OpenAI also notes that ChatGPT may still use limited information from prior conversations for safety and security in rare, high-risk situations. Turning off memory and personalization doesn't disable those safety features.

What happens when you save it

You can save a temporary chat to your history if you want to return to it later. Saving turns it into a regular chat, even if it started Unpersonalized.

From that point it follows your account's personalization and model-improvement settings. If Memory is on, the saved conversation may be used to personalize future responses. If you don't save it, it won't appear in your history and you can't reopen it from history after you leave.

If the temporary chat had uploaded files, eligible ones may also be saved to Library or Space when you save the chat, subject to availability and storage limits.

Model training and retention

While a chat remains temporary, OpenAI says it is not used to improve models, whether or not you chose personalization. If you save it, your model-improvement setting applies.

OpenAI may keep a copy of a temporary chat for up to 30 days for safety purposes. Once saved, the conversation follows the retention rules for saved chats.

Temporary vs regular chats

  • History: temporary stays out of history unless you save it; regular chats stay until you delete them (or a workspace policy removes them).
  • Return later: save a temporary chat to reopen it; regular chats reopen from history or Search.
  • Memory: a personalized temporary chat can use existing memories but won't create or update them; regular chats can use and create memories when Memory is on.
  • Model training: temporary chats aren't used to improve models while temporary; saved chats follow your model-improvement setting.
  • Best for: temporary when you don't want the chat in history unless you choose to keep it; regular when you want to keep and return to it.

Temporary chat with GPTs

You can use temporary chat with GPTs. OpenAI notes that if a GPT uses actions, data sent to a third party through an action follows that recipient's privacy policy, which may retain the data longer than 30 days.

When temporary chat helps (and when it doesn't)

Use temporary chat when you're about to paste something sensitive, try a one-off question you don't want in history, or want a session that won't write new memories. Choose Unpersonalized if you also don't want existing memories or custom instructions applied.

It doesn't replace a clear personal context file. Temporary chat is a session mode; your role, projects and preferences still live better in Custom Instructions or a file you own.
