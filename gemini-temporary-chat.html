What is a temporary chat in Gemini?

A temporary chat is a Gemini conversation that won't appear in your recent chats or your Gemini Apps Activity, and isn't used for personalization or to train Google's AI models. Here's how it works, based on Google's own help pages.

Menu names and options can change, and some may not be in your account yet. Treat the steps below as signposts.

Who can use it

Google's Gemini Apps Help lists three requirements:

  • You're signed in to Gemini Apps.
  • You're using a personal Google Account. The consumer help page says the feature isn't available to work or school accounts.
  • You're in the Gemini web or mobile app. It isn't available in Gemini in Chrome or Gemini in Google Messages.

On work and school accounts, Google Workspace Help describes an admin setting, Allow temporary chats, that controls whether people in the organization can start them. If your organization uses Google Vault for Gemini, Vault retention rules still apply to temporary chats, and Gemini shows a message saying your activity may still be visible to your admins or Vault users.

How to start a temporary chat

  1. On your computer, go to gemini.google.com.
  2. Next to New chat, click Temporary chat.
  3. Type your question or prompt in the box at the bottom and submit it.

While you're in one, a temporary chat icon shows at the top of the screen. When you leave, you can no longer get back to that chat, and it won't show in your recent chats.

What you can't do in a temporary chat

Google lists these limits. In a temporary chat you can't:

  • Use Gems.
  • Use Connected Apps and other services that need the Keep Activity setting turned on.
  • Get personalized responses.
  • Save information for future personalization.
  • Give feedback on a response.

So a temporary chat starts without your personalization, and nothing you say in it is kept for later. Google's privacy hub also suggests temporary chats for anything you don't want Gemini to remember or use to personalize your experience.

What Google keeps, and for how long

According to the Gemini Apps Privacy Hub:

  • Temporary chats are kept with your account for 72 hours. Google uses them to respond to you (using the last 24 hours of the chat as context) and to help protect Google, its users and the public, for example in case of a system failure.
  • Temporary chats are not used to train Google's AI models, and they aren't used to improve Google AI with help from human reviewers.
  • Even in a temporary chat, Google still uses your chats to respond to you and to help keep people safe, including with help from human reviewers.

Temporary chat vs turning off Keep Activity

A temporary chat covers one conversation. Turning off the Keep Activity setting affects all your future chats: they won't appear in your activity and won't be used to train Google's models unless you send feedback, but they're still kept for 72 hours. A temporary chat is the lighter option when you only want one conversation kept out.

When temporary chat helps (and when it doesn't)

Use it for a quick one-off question, something you'd rather not see in your history, or a topic you don't want shaping Gemini's memory of you. Since there's no way back in after you leave, copy anything you need before you close it.

It's not a way to give Gemini context. For saved preferences, see Instructions for Gemini; for what Gemini already knows from past chats, see how Gemini Memory works. If you use ChatGPT too, its version works differently: see temporary chat in ChatGPT.

Because a temporary chat skips personalization, a personal context file you control is the simplest way to give it the background you want. Paste it at the start of the chat with this line:

Here is my personal context file. Read it and use it for everything in this chat. You don't need to summarize it back to me. Just reply "Got it" and wait for my first request.

[paste your context file here]

Sources: Gemini Apps Help, Use Gemini Apps (Start a temporary chat) and Gemini Apps Privacy Hub; Google Workspace Help, Control temporary chats and chat deletion in the Gemini App.
