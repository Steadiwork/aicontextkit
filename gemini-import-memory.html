How to move your ChatGPT or Claude memory to Gemini

If you're switching to Gemini from ChatGPT, Claude or another AI, you don't have to start over. Gemini can import two things: your memory (preferences, facts and context the other AI has learned about you) and, separately, your full chat history, so you can continue old conversations in Gemini. Here's how both work, based on Google's own help page.

Menu names and options can change, and some may not be in your account yet. Treat the steps below as signposts.

Who can use it

Google's Gemini Apps Help lists these requirements:

  • You're signed in with a personal Google Account. Import isn't available on work, school or supervised accounts.
  • You still have access to the other AI platform with your saved chats and memory.
  • You're 18 or older.

Google says the feature isn't currently available in the EEA, Switzerland or the United Kingdom, or in Gemini in Google Messages, Gemini in Chrome or Gemini on Android XR. The steps below are for gemini.google.com on a computer.

Import your memory (the quick option)

This moves a written summary of what your old AI knows about you, not your chats. Gemini gives you the prompt to use:

  1. Go to gemini.google.com.
  2. At the bottom, click Settings, then Import memory to Gemini.
  3. Copy the prompt Gemini provides.
  4. Open the AI you're leaving (ChatGPT, Claude or another) and paste the prompt into a chat.
  5. Copy its response, the part that lists memories about you.
  6. Back in Gemini, paste it into the text field and click Add memory.

Google says Gemini then creates a new chat thread and works this content in so it can "remember" these details in future conversations.

Before you paste, read the response and delete anything you don't want Gemini to have, such as health details, account numbers or anything about other people. Your old AI's summary can also be out of date, so it's worth a quick check.

Import your full chat history

If you want your old conversations too, you can upload an export file. Google says this can take up to a day, depending on the platform you're exporting from.

1. Download your history from the other AI. Google's help page gives these steps:

  • ChatGPT: click your username at the bottom left, then Settings > Data controls. Next to "Export data," click Export, then Confirm Export.
  • Claude: click your username at the bottom left, then Settings > Privacy. Next to "Export data," click Export, choose the date range, then click Export.

Either way, a download link is sent to the email address on that account.

2. Upload it to Gemini. Go to gemini.google.com, click Settings at the bottom, then Import memory to Gemini. Under "Import chats," click Add and pick the .zip file. Limits Google lists:

  • File type: .zip only.
  • File size: up to 5 GB.
  • Up to 5 .zip files per day.

Find, delete or redo imported chats

  • Find them: open the menu at the top left. Under "Chats," imported chats have an imported-chat icon. You can also search your chat history.
  • Delete one: find the chat, click More, then Delete.
  • Delete a whole import: next to the import entry, click Delete. That removes every chat from that .zip file.
  • Upload again: re-uploading an export adds any new conversations and overwrites the ones you imported before.
  • Partial import: if an import is interrupted, Gemini tells you and some chats may be only partly imported. Google's fix is to delete the import entry and try again.

How Google handles what you import

This is the part to read before you upload years of chats. Google says imported chats, and chats you continue in Gemini, are saved in your Activity, and that this data is used to improve its services (including training generative AI models) and to protect Google, its users and the public. You can manage or delete that activity at any time, and everything you import falls under the Google Privacy Policy. If you'd rather not hand over your full history, the memory import alone moves much less.

After the import: check what Gemini kept

Once your memory is in, see how Gemini Memory works to review or correct what it remembers. For things you want applied to every chat, such as your role or how you like answers, put them in Instructions for Gemini rather than relying on an imported summary. Moving the other way? Claude has its own version: how to move your ChatGPT memory to Claude.

The simplest way to never be stuck again is a personal context file you write once and own. It works in Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude and Grok, so the next time you switch you just paste it in. In any new chat, paste this line with your file underneath:

Here is my personal context file. Read it and use it for everything in this chat. You don't need to summarize it back to me. Just reply "Got it" and wait for my first request.

[paste your context file here]
